서강젊은광고인네트워크

Do you love advertising?

If you've ever paused in front of a subway billboard for no reason,
or watched a YouTube ad to the end instead of skipping it,
we're probably alike.

I made this page to talk with people like that.

But loving advertising as a liberal-arts student here
can feel stuck more often than you'd think.

How long will it be just supporter programs?

How many card-news posts with no views
have you put on your own account?

The KPIs don't come,
and it's hard to fit into a single line on a résumé.

The idea you sent to a contest
wins one award and goes into a drawer.

You want to see your idea actually out in the world,
but no company will take it,
and reaching out first feels scary.

I was the same.

I was born in 2006, and I still have a lot to learn.

Like everyone else, I joined student supporter programs,
did some research for a while,
made public-service ads and put them up myself,
and earned certificates.

Then, at my very first startup competition, I was lucky enough to win a Minister’s Award.

That led me to a political-marketing startup.
I pitched directly to members of the National Assembly,
ran content marketing and shot videos with them.
They reached millions of views,
and videos I made even appeared on the news.

I left to do more of what I learned there with my own hands,
and now I run a small ad agency I founded.

On social media, I’ve made content with over 9 million views.

Our outdoor ads in Seongsu were featured in magazines,
and at the end of this year, ads made by our team
will run in Gangnam subway stations and on buses.

I've filmed videos with YouTubers I'd only seen on TV,
and planned events with them.

I don't think this is anything grand.
I was lucky, and I met good people.
I just wanted to gently share that even as a student, even in the liberal arts,
you can put an ad out into the world.

What SYAN becomes,
I want to shape together with the people who join.
But one thing I firmly believe.

Just by bringing together people with a real sense for advertising,
striking against each other like flint, sparks will fly,
and those small sparks might change the world a little.

If we keep having fun conversations together,
something good is bound to come, right?

So SYAN isn't an extracurricular program.
It's not a club, and it's not a team formed to do projects.

It's simply one group chat.

There we casually share talk about advertising, marketing, and branding,
good ads we've seen lately,
and ideas that just won't leave our heads.

And when something good comes up now and then,
that’s when we do a project together.

No fixed schedule, no required tasks.
You can easily do it alongside your classes.

We'd like to stack those conversations and ideas into a database,
and someday even try AI transformation (AX) with it.

It's nothing grand.
I'd just like us to think about thinking, together.
I hope people interested in this will gather.

Still, carefully, there is one thing I hope for.

Someone who truly loves advertising,
who wants to compete with their own colors rather than the usual route,
and who can't stop thinking.

It's okay if you're not outstanding yet.
I want to work with people who have clear potential inside.
Honestly, I hope many people better than me will come.

Though I have much to learn, I want to help as much as I know and can.
I'm also planning sessions with award winners from international ad festivals.

It would be an honor
to sketch the first draft of your advertising career together.

No résumé needed. Just tell me your story.
I'll read every one myself, carefully.
